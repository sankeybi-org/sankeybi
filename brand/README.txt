SANKEYBI BRAND ARTWORK
=====================

Colours
  Water / petrol   #005F73   (main brand colour)
  RFNBO / teal     #2AA7A3   (the petrol's lighter partner on dark backgrounds)
  Electricity/salmon #FF8579 (the second stripe, always)
  Page light       #F5F8FB
  Page dark        #0D141B

Energy palette: Solar #FFE066, Wind #A5D8FF, Water #005F73, Biofuel #B2F2BB,
RFNBO #2AA7A3, Nuclear #7950F2, Fossil #343A40, Electricity #FF8579, Heat #B5231F

Five versions of each piece of artwork
  light              petrol + salmon on off-white
  dark               teal + salmon on near-black
  petrol             pale + salmon on a petrol tile / petrol background
  light-transparent  petrol + salmon, no background (use on light pages)
  dark-transparent   teal + salmon, no background (use on dark pages)

Folders
  icon/      the square S. SVG for every version, PNG at 20, 40, 300, 512 px
             (the transparent versions at 512 px)
  wordmark/  the full "SankeyBI" name. SVG and PNG for every version
  power-bi/  icon.png (20 x 20, for the visual), appsource-logo-300.png,
             and Power BI report themes (light and dark)
  favicon/   favicon.svg, favicon-32.png, apple-touch-icon.png (180 px)
  hero/      Power BI screenshot used at the top of the website
